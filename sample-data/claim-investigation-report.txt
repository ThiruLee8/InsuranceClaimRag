1. SUMMARY
----------
This investigation concerns a residential homeowners claim for sudden water damage.
The insured reported that a supply line to an upstairs bathroom failed overnight,
allowing water to travel through the ceiling into the kitchen and living room.

2. CAUSE OF LOSS
----------------
The cause of the loss was a burst flexible supply line connected to the upstairs
bathroom faucet. Corrosion and material fatigue were observed at the failure point.
There is no indication of gradual seepage prior to the failure date. The loss is
classified as sudden and accidental water discharge.

3. DAMAGE ASSESSMENT
--------------------
Affected areas:
- Kitchen ceiling and cabinetry
- Living room hardwood flooring (approx. 180 sq ft)
- Upstairs bathroom vanity and adjacent drywall

Estimated repair cost from contractor estimate EST-4412: USD 18,450.
Personal property damaged includes a dining table and electronic equipment totaling
USD 2,100 based on receipts provided by the insured.

4. COVERAGE ANALYSIS
--------------------
Policy HO-998877-55 provides Coverage A (Dwelling) and Coverage C (Personal Property).
A deductible of USD 1,000 applies. No evidence of excluded causes (flood, neglect, or
intentional damage) was found during the inspection.

5. RECOMMENDATION
-----------------
Recommend approval of covered repairs subject to deductible, pending final review of
contractor invoices and proof of ownership for personal property items.
