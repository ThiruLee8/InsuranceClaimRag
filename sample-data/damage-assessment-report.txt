DAMAGE ASSESSMENT REPORT
========================

Claim Number: CLM-2024-00847
Assessment Date: March 18, 2024
Assessor: M. Chen, Field Surveyor

Property Impact Overview
------------------------
Water migrated from the second-floor bathroom through ceiling cavities into the
kitchen and living room. Moisture meter readings exceeded 25% in affected drywall.

Room-by-Room Findings
---------------------
Bathroom:
- Vanity base swollen
- Floor underlayment compromised near vanity

Kitchen:
- Ceiling drywall requires replacement (approx. 64 sq ft)
- Upper cabinets show water staining and swelling

Living Room:
- Hardwood cupping across approximately 180 sq ft
- Baseboards along north wall require replacement

Cause Confirmation
------------------
Physical inspection confirms a failed flexible faucet supply line as the origin of
the water release. No roof leaks or exterior water intrusion were identified.

Recommended Remediation
-----------------------
1. Water extraction and drying (completed March 14-16)
2. Replace bathroom vanity and adjacent drywall
3. Replace kitchen ceiling and damaged cabinetry
4. Refinish or replace living room hardwood as needed

Preliminary Cost Range: USD 16,800 - USD 19,200
