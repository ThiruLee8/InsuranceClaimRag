The app watches itself

Every answer is a trace. Every step has its own time and cost. A vague complaint can be looked up, and a real miss becomes a test that stays.

How the mentor checks this
  1. Find one past answer from a vague complaint, without reading the log by hand.
  2. The log shows time and cost per step, and the total is the sum of those steps.
  3. Cost per request was measured, then cut with a prompt cache, a semantic cache, and a cheaper model for easy questions.
  4. A real miss (the invented investigator) is now a test. The bad answer fails it. The corrected answer passes.
